Half Adder Architecture

The Half Adder is a combinational arithmetic circuit that performs addition of two single binary bits (A and B). It produces two outputs: SUM (representing the unit bit) and CARRY (representing the overflow to the next significant position). It has no provision for an incoming carry bit.

Sum Bit: SUM = A ⊕ B  (XOR Gate)
Carry Bit: CARRY = A · B  (AND Gate)
3 Inputs · 5 Gates CASCADE CAPABLE

Full Adder Architecture

The Full Adder adds three 1-bit binary values: operands A, B, and a Carry-in (Cin) generated by the preceding adder stage. Composed of two Half Adders and an OR gate, it allows cascading into multi-bit Parallel Ripple-Carry Adders used in computer arithmetic logic units.

Sum Bit: SUM = A ⊕ B ⊕ Cin
Carry-Out: Cout = (A·B) + (Cin·(A⊕B))

Outputs HIGH (1) only when all inputs are 1. Detects arithmetic carry generation.

Outputs HIGH (1) if at least one input is 1. Combines carry paths in Full Adder.
